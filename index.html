1. The Core Principle

The App is built to be privacy-friendly and useful without unnecessary accounts or tracking.

  • No account is required to use the App.
  • No third-party ads are shown in the App.
  • The App is designed without a developer-run cloud profile for users.
  • The majority of your data, including scan history, saved product notes, local profiles, allergen preferences, digestive-profile preferences, keto preferences, Healthy Longevity preferences, fiber tracking entries, scan mode, selected translation language, voice settings, reminder settings, reduced-motion settings, and most other settings, is stored only on your device.
  • The App does not sell your personal data.
  • Some features depend on third-party services such as product databases, nutrition databases, translation services, speech services, cosmetic/chemical reference sources, donation pages, app stores, and your device's built-in camera, speech, and notification services. Those services may receive limited data needed to provide the feature you choose to use.

2. Information Processed on Your Device

To provide its features, the App needs to access or process certain information. Most of this is handled locally on your device.

a. Camera, photos, barcode scanning, and manual scanning

  • Why we need it: The App supports barcode scanning and manual product scanning from photos, including human food ingredient lists, pet food ingredient lists, cosmetic INCI lists, and cleaning product labels.
  • How it is used: The camera is only used when you actively scan a product, search for a barcode, or take a photo for text recognition.
  • Camera guidance: When supported, the App may use barcode boxes, visible text blocks, and screen-position math to provide guidance such as moving left, right, up, down, closer, or holding steady. This guidance is an accessibility and usability feature and does not identify you.
  • On-device processing: Depending on platform and app version, barcode scanning, text recognition, language identification, image crop assistance, and related features may use Google ML Kit on Android and/or Apple system frameworks and translation-related system services on iPhone.
  • Temporary files: Temporary image files, crop data, or cache files may be created during scan processing or image handling on your device.

b. Scan history and product results

  • The App can save recently scanned products so you can view them again later.
  • Saved scan history is stored only in the App's local device storage.
  • You can disable history saving or clear saved history in the App settings.

c. Per-product notes, safe list, favorites, shelf-style saving, and saved reminders

  • You can save your own notes for scanned products, for example packaging details, missing allergen information, digestive triggers, sensitivities, or personal reminders.
  • The App may include local product saving features such as safe list, favorites, shelf-style product saving, or product notes.
  • These saved items are stored locally on your device only, usually per barcode or per scanned product key.
  • Notes and saved products are not shared with other users and are not uploaded by the App to a developer-operated account.

d. Local profiles and preferences

  • The App may let users create or switch local profiles, for example for different family members or different preference sets.
  • Profile names, selected allergens, sensitivities, digestive profiles, keto settings, Healthy Longevity settings, custom digestive triggers, Biofarm / Cleaner Label preferences, nutrition alert toggles, scan mode, translation language, voice mode, speech rate and pitch, dark mode, background theme, notification preferences, reduced-motion preferences, and similar settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Profiles are local convenience features. They are not online accounts.

e. Translation settings and cache

  • The App may remember your selected target translation language so future scanned products and supported App screens can be translated more conveniently.
  • The selected language and any cached translations are stored locally on your device.
  • You can switch back to Original text or change translation settings inside the App.
  • Official product names, brand names, barcode database names, scientific names, and cosmetic INCI names may stay in their original language because translating those terms can make them less safe or less recognizable.

f. Raw food, fiber, nutrition, vitamins, and minerals

  • The App may include raw food search, fiber lookup, nutrition lookup, daily fiber tracking, vitamin notes, mineral notes, or similar diet-support tools.
  • Food names, selected amounts, daily totals, and diary-style entries are stored locally unless a feature clearly uses an online database lookup.
  • Vitamin and mineral information is shown only when available from product data or supported local/reference data. It is informational only.

g. Experimental product score, baskets, and warnings

  • The App may show an experimental LB System Rating, such as an out-of-100 score, based on available product data, warnings, allergens, ingredients, nutrition details, and user settings.
  • The App may include basket-style summaries for scanned products, such as Healthy Longevity, Blue Zone, FODMAP, IBS/SIBO, keto, or household safety style summaries where available.
  • Scores, baskets, and related explanations are generated for convenience and are informational only.
  • Scores may be incomplete or wrong if product data is incomplete, outdated, mistranslated, or missing.

h. Ingredient insights and ingredient relations

  • The App may show ingredient summaries, aliases, ingredient families, related ingredients, source/origin notes, INCI position notes for cosmetics, sensitivity flags, and cross-mode warnings across food, cosmetics, pet food, and cleaning products where data is available.
  • These insights are based on local data, public references, database information, product labels, and app rules. Coverage varies and may be incomplete.

i. Product images and cached media

  • If a product database provides an image URL, the App may load that image and cache it locally on your device for display performance.

j. App health and diagnostics

  • The App may show an App Health screen with information such as camera permission, notification permission, speech-service availability, selected language, translator setting, reminder status, reduced-motion status, local database counts, and recent online-request status.
  • This information is shown to help users understand whether features can work correctly on their device.
  • App Health information is not a developer-run diagnostic upload and is not an account profile.

3. Voice, Speech, and Accessibility Features

The App may include optional voice and accessibility features. Availability depends on your device, installed speech services, permissions, language, and app version.

a. Text-to-speech / spoken results

  • If you enable Voice Assistant Mode, the App can send text to your device's configured speech engine so results, warnings, ingredients, guidance, and answers can be read aloud.
  • On Android this may use Android Text-to-Speech, Google Speech Services, or another installed device speech engine. On iPhone this may use Apple speech services or another supported system service.
  • The App does not create its own custom cloud voice. The voice normally comes from the device or operating system.

b. Spoken questions and voice profile selection

  • If you use spoken product questions or voice profile selection, your device's speech-recognition service may process your voice input and return recognized text to the App.
  • Depending on your device and settings, speech recognition may be handled locally or by a system/provider service such as Google or Apple. That provider may process voice input under its own privacy policy and terms.
  • The App uses the recognized text to answer questions from the currently available scan data, selected local profile, and app rules. It does not guarantee safety.
  • The App does not require a developer-run account to use these voice features.

c. Not a generative-AI chatbot

  • The App's spoken product answers are based on available scan data, local JSON data, selected profiles, product database data, and app logic.
  • The App does not send your product questions to ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenAI, IBM, or a developer-operated large language model service.
  • Machine-learning services may still be used for OCR/text recognition, barcode support, language identification, translation, or platform speech features, depending on platform and settings.

d. Warning tones and camera guidance

  • The App may play an optional urgent warning tone before severe matched profile warnings are spoken.
  • The App may provide spoken camera guidance for barcode scanning or ingredient-label scanning. This is based on detected barcode/text position and may be wrong if the camera view is blurry, dark, tilted, blocked, or too far away.

4. Information Shared With Third-Party Services

Some features require limited data to be sent to third-party services. We do not require an account from you to use these features.

a. Barcode lookups

  • Human food: When you scan a human food barcode, the barcode may be sent to Open Food Facts and Pro Open Food Facts to retrieve product information.
  • If you manually enable the optional legacy source where available, the barcode may also be sent to FoodRepo.org.
  • Pet food: When you scan a pet food barcode, the barcode may be sent to Open Pet Food Facts.
  • Cosmetics: When you scan a cosmetic barcode, the barcode may be sent to Open Beauty Facts.
  • Cleaning products: When you scan a cleaning product barcode, the barcode may be sent to Open Products Facts.

b. Universal / Auto Detect scanner

  • If you use Universal / Auto Detect mode, the barcode may be checked against multiple supported barcode databases one by one until matching product data is found or sources are exhausted.
  • This mode may be slower and may send the barcode to more than one supported product database.

c. Product search by name

  • If you search for a product by name, the search text may be sent to the selected or relevant product database service so matching products can be shown.
  • Search results depend on the third-party database and may be incomplete or unavailable.

d. Raw food and nutrition lookups

  • If the App offers raw food, fiber, vitamin, mineral, or nutrition lookup from online sources, the food name or search query may be sent to public nutrition databases or reference sources, such as USDA FoodData Central or other open/public food data providers where supported.
  • The App may use returned nutrition information to show informational results.

e. Translation services

  • Depending on platform, app version, and chosen settings, translation may use Google ML Kit, Apple Translate or related Apple system translation services, LibreTranslate, or Lingva Translate.
  • If you choose an online translator, the text you choose to translate may be sent to that selected third-party service.
  • Some translation models or language packages may be downloaded to your device when needed.

f. Speech services

  • If you use spoken questions, voice profile selection, text-to-speech, or similar voice features, your device's installed speech services may process speech input, recognized text, or spoken output under their own privacy policies and terms.
  • The App only uses the speech result needed for the feature you actively choose to use.

g. Cosmetic ingredient research features

  • If you tap a cosmetic ingredient card to load extra reference data, the ingredient name may be sent to PubChem to retrieve chemical reference information.
  • If you choose to open CosIng, ECHA, or EWG Skin Deep, the App may open those services in your browser. Your use of those sites is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

h. Source product pages and contribution websites

  • If you choose to open a product's source page or use a contribution option, the App may open an external website in your browser and include the barcode, product code, or search query in the destination URL.

i. Optional donations / support

  • If you tap the Donate option, the App may open PayPal or Wise in your browser or app so you can support the creator voluntarily.
  • Donations are optional and do not unlock premium features, faster scans, badges, or digital extras inside the App.
  • The App does not process your payment card or banking details directly. Payment information is handled by the selected donation provider.

j. Email contact and suggestions

  • If you choose to send us an email, your email app/provider will process your email address and message contents in the normal way.
  • We will receive whatever information you voluntarily include in that email and use it to respond, investigate the issue, provide support, review suggestions, or improve the App.

k. App stores and platform services

  • Google Play, the Apple App Store, Android, iOS, and device manufacturers may process information such as installs, updates, crashes, diagnostics, reviews, ratings, or store interactions under their own privacy policies.
  • This is separate from the App's local storage and from any developer-operated account, because the App does not require an account.

5. No Account, No Ads, Optional Support

  • The App does not show third-party ads.
  • The App is designed to work without a cloud profile or centralized user account.
  • Optional donations may be offered through external services, but they do not change the App's core functionality or grant digital benefits.

7. Important Accuracy, Allergy, Digestive, Diet, Voice, and Safety Notice

  • The App relies in part on third-party and community-maintained product databases. Product information can be incomplete, outdated, mistranslated, or inaccurate.
  • A valid real-world product may not yet appear in a free public database, even if its barcode is correct.
  • Allergen, ingredient, digestive-trigger, keto, fiber, nutrition, vitamin, mineral, cosmetic, pet food, or cleaning-product information may be missing or incomplete in online database entries.
  • The App may generate warnings, ingredient insights, translations, voice summaries, spoken answers, camera guidance, digestive-profile alerts, keto-related alerts, cleaner-label alerts, raw food/fiber estimates, note-based reminders, or experimental product scores, but these are informational only.
  • Spoken answers are limited to the information available in the current scan and App data. They are not a guarantee that a product is safe, suitable, complete, or recommended.
  • Camera guidance can fail or be inaccurate if the product is blurry, poorly lit, too close, too far, tilted, damaged, reflective, partially hidden, or not supported by the scanner.
  • The experimental score out of 100 is a convenience feature. It is not a guarantee that a product is safe, healthy, suitable, recommended, or appropriate for any specific person, pet, condition, allergy, diet, or household.
  • Always check the real product packaging and manufacturer information, especially for allergies, intolerances, IBS / SIBO / digestive sensitivities, keto or other diet restrictions, medical conditions, pet safety, chemical exposure concerns, or skin sensitivities.
  • The App does not provide medical, nutritional, veterinary, dermatological, toxicological, regulatory, or legal advice.
  • Cosmetic and chemical reference tools such as PubChem, CosIng, ECHA, and EWG are provided for research convenience only and may have their own limitations, notices, and rules.
Important: If you have a serious allergy, medical condition, dietary restriction, or pet-safety concern, do not rely on the App alone. Check the product label and ask a qualified professional where appropriate.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Camera Permission: You can deny or revoke camera access at any time in system settings.
  • Microphone / Speech Input: If your platform requests microphone or speech-recognition permission for spoken questions or voice profile selection, you can deny or revoke it in system settings. Availability depends on your device and speech provider.
  • Notification Permission: You can disable scan reminders in the App settings or revoke notification permission in system settings.
  • History Controls: You can disable history saving and clear saved scan history inside the App.
  • Notes and Preferences: You can edit or clear your local product notes, family/profile settings, allergen settings, sensitivity settings, digestive profiles, keto settings, fiber tracking entries, translation language, voice settings, and other settings inside the App.
  • Translation Choice: You can choose which translation service the App uses when multiple options are offered.
  • Voice Controls: You can disable voice mode, stop speech, adjust speech settings, or avoid spoken-question features at any time.
  • Database Controls: Where the App offers database options, you can adjust them in settings.
  • Reduced Motion: You can enable reduced-motion settings where supported to limit screen transition animations.
  • Local Data Removal: You can remove local App data by clearing App storage or uninstalling the App.
